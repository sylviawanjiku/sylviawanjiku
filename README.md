### Hi, I'm Sylvia 👋

I'm a engineering manager/ software engineer at [Jacaranda Health](https://jacarandahealth.org). Mothers shouldn't die because care came too late. I build the tech that helps close that gap across Kenya, Ghana, Nigeria and Tanzania.

**🛠️ What I work on**

- **Backend APIs and data**: services that manage enrollment, consent and programme data for mothers on our SMS and chat platforms (Python, PostgreSQL)
- **Messaging at scale**: RapidPro integrations and migrations that keep health messages flowing as we grow into new countries
- **AI/ML and MLOps**: NLP models that route messages from mothers to the right support, plus the pipelines to train, deploy, monitor and retrain them in production
- **Internal tools**: forms, task tracking and infrastructure that give our clinical and programme teams their time back

**🧪 Side quests**
