### Hi, I'm Sylvia 👋

I'm a software engineer at [Jacaranda Health](https://jacarandahealth.org), where I build the digital tools behind maternal and newborn health programs in Kenya, Ghana, Nigeria and Tanzania.

**What I work on**

- **Backend APIs and data**: services that manage enrollment, consent and programme data for mothers using our SMS and chat platforms (Python, PostgreSQL)
- **Messaging at scale**: RapidPro integrations and migrations that keep health messages flowing as we expand to new countries
- **AI/ML for health**: models and pipelines that help route messages from mothers to the right support
- **Internal tools**: forms, task tracking and infrastructure that help our clinical and programme teams move faster

**Side projects**

- **fhir-ai-agent-system** (private, in progress): a self-hosted AI agent system that turns legacy health records into FHIR-compliant resources
- [whimsy-themes](https://github.com/sylviawanjiku/whimsy-themes): pastel colour themes for VS Code and Cursor
- [side-quests](https://github.com/sylviawanjiku/side-quests): a whimsical kanban for side projects (web app and Chrome extension)

**Interests**: digital health, interoperability (FHIR), NLP, and tools that make engineers' days a little nicer.
